# Production Systems – Mass Production System Design for a Pallet Transporter

## Overview

This project was developed during the Production Systems course as part of the Mechanical Engineering programme.
Rather than receiving predefined manufacturing data, students were assigned photographs of commercial products and challenged to work backwards through the engineering design process.
The objective was to identify the product, study its construction, determine how each component could be manufactured, and design a complete mass production system capable of manufacturing the product efficiently.
The final result was not only the design of the manufacturing process, but the complete conceptual design of an industrial facility.

## Project Background

The assigned product was a **Pallet Transporter**.

Starting only from images of the product, the project involved:

- Identifying every major component;
- Determining suitable manufacturing processes;
- Selecting production equipment;
- Defining the production sequence;
- Planning material flow;
- Designing a mass production system;
- Developing the complete factory layout.

The project simulates one of the fundamental responsibilities of manufacturing engineers: transforming a commercial product into a complete industrial production system.

## Engineering Concepts Covered

This project integrates several topics from Manufacturing and Industrial Engineering, including:

- Product decomposition
- Manufacturing process selection
- Machine selection
- Production balancing
- Capacity planning
- Material flow analysis
- Facility layout planning
- Warehouse sizing
- Industrial building design
- Utilities planning
- Production logistics

## Factory Design

Beyond production planning, the project also included the conceptual design of the manufacturing facility.

Engineering decisions included:

- Factory location
- Production layout
- Material flow
- Internal transportation
- Warehouse organization
- Production sectors
- Quality control area
- Painting and drying sectors
- Employee facilities
- Administrative offices
- Building dimensions
- Floor selection
- Wall construction
- Roof configuration
- Lighting
- Ventilation
- Energy supply
- Water storage

The objective was to create a production system capable of supporting efficient mass manufacturing while considering operational and infrastructure requirements.
